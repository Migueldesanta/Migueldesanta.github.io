About

I am a quantitative researcher trained at Penn State University in Statistics and Political Science. My work brings rigorous causal-inference methods — difference-in-differences, threshold regression, instrumental variables, event studies — to questions in fixed income, monetary policy, and computational social science.

I am currently with Nittan Securities Asia Limited in Hong Kong, where I produce sovereign-bond and Federal Reserve policy research, and build the dashboards that turn the research into something a desk can actually use.
